❓ How to Use This App — in plain words

One customer = one journey. You just walk them through 5 steps, left to right. Everything hangs on the Customer ID — the app links everything for you.

1
🪪 Add the customer (KYC tab)
Press ➕ New Lead (top right) or ➕ New KYC. Fill name + company at minimum — the rest can be filled later. The Customer ID (e.g. CUST-0003) is created automatically. Never make one up yourself.
2
💬 Answer their questions (Q&A Inbox tab)
Every question the customer asks — by call, WhatsApp, email — gets logged and answered here. Click a row, type the reply, save. It appears in the customer's window automatically.
3
💳 Confirm their payment (Payments tab)
When the customer pays the R&D / sampling fee (handled in CRM / accounts), mark it confirmed here. No payment = no sampling — the app locks it for you, so you can't go wrong.
4
🧪 Make & track samples (Samples tab)
Create a sample from the customer's query + formula. Then just keep its status up to date: Requested → In Lab → Sent → Feedback → Approved. That's it.
5
🧾 Order created (Orders tab)
The moment a sample is Approved, an Order ID is auto-created — inheriting the customer baseline via the Customer ID master anchor. This Orders tab is the same shared ledger the production hub executes from: Customer ID → Order ID → Fulfilment.
🔑 3 golden rules:
① Click any blue ID (CUST-, QRY-, FORM-, SMPL-) anywhere — it opens the full detail window for that item. A Customer ID always opens the same full window with all 7 tabs: Overview, Q&A, Products, Formulas, Samples, Payments, Approvals.
② Follow the steps 1 → 5 in order. If something looks locked 🔒, it means an earlier step isn't done yet — usually the payment.
③ Hover any button to see a one-line explanation of what it does.
